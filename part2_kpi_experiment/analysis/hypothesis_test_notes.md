# Hypothesis Test Notes

## Primary Metric Tested

Paid Conversion Rate

## Business Decision

Determine whether the new onboarding and activation campaign should be launched to all users.

## Null Hypothesis (H0)

The Treatment group's paid conversion rate is less than or equal to the Control group's paid conversion rate.

H0: Treatment Conversion Rate ≤ Control Conversion Rate

## Alternative Hypothesis (H1)

The Treatment group's paid conversion rate is higher than the Control group's paid conversion rate.

H1: Treatment Conversion Rate > Control Conversion Rate

## Test Type

One-tailed A/B test for conversion improvement.

## Significance Level

Alpha = 0.05

## Control Group Result

Paid Conversion Rate = 3.17%

## Treatment Group Result

Paid Conversion Rate = 6.99%

## Decision Rule

Reject the null hypothesis if the evidence shows that the Treatment group achieved a meaningful improvement in paid conversion over the Control group.

## Business Interpretation

The Treatment group achieved a substantially higher paid conversion rate compared to the Control group.

Control Conversion Rate: 3.17%
Treatment Conversion Rate: 6.99%

This represents an increase of approximately 120% relative improvement.

The experiment provides sufficient business evidence to support launching the new onboarding experience while continuing to monitor guardrail metrics such as refunds and support tickets.
