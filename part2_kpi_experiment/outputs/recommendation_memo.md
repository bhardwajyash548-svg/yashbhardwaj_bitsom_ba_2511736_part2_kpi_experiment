# Recommendation Memo

# Executive Summary

The company tested a new onboarding and activation campaign to improve user conversion and early engagement.

The experiment compared the existing onboarding experience (Control group) with the new campaign experience (Treatment group).

Results indicate that the Treatment experience outperformed the Control experience across the primary business metrics.

# North Star Metric

Paid Conversion Rate

The North Star Metric was selected because it directly measures business growth and subscription revenue generation.

# KPI Tree Summary

Primary KPI Drivers:

* Landing Page Visit Rate
* Trial Start Rate
* Onboarding Completion Rate

Guardrail Metrics:

* Refund Rate
* Support Ticket Rate
* Average Days to Convert
* Engagement Score

# Experiment Results

| Metric                     | Control | Treatment |
| -------------------------- | ------- | --------- |
| Paid Conversion Rate       | 3.17%   | 6.99%     |
| Landing Page Visit Rate    | 63.64%  | 72.59%    |
| Trial Start Rate           | 25.11%  | 29.09%    |
| Onboarding Completion Rate | 15.58%  | 21.26%    |
| ARPU                       | 51.75   | 53.88     |
| Engagement Score           | 57.03   | 62.93     |

# Hypothesis Test Interpretation

The Treatment group delivered a meaningful increase in paid conversion and therefore the business objective of improving monetization was achieved.

# Guardrail Analysis

* Refund rate increased slightly from 0.00% to 0.42%.
* Support ticket rate increased from 21.93% to 37.20%.
* Engagement score improved significantly.
* Revenue quality remained stable.

Although support tickets increased, the increase is manageable and does not outweigh the conversion gains.

# Segment Insights

* Treatment outperformed Control across all regions.
* The campaign showed strong performance across different customer segments.
* No major segment experienced performance decline.

# Final Recommendation

Launch to all users.

The increase in paid conversion, engagement, and revenue outweighs the moderate increase in support tickets and refunds.

# Risks and Limitations

* Support ticket volume should continue to be monitored.
* Longer-term retention impact has not yet been measured.
* Results are based on the current experiment population only.

# Next Steps

1. Launch the new onboarding experience to all users.
2. Continue monitoring guardrail metrics.
3. Measure retention and long-term revenue impact.
4. Run follow-up experiments to optimize onboarding further.
